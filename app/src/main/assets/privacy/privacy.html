Privacy Policy

Last updated: February 2026

1. Information We Collect

AstroApp collects the following information to provide personalized astrology readings:

  • Account Information: Name, email address (provided during sign-up)
  • Reading Data: Birth details (date, time, place), questions asked, and reading history
  • Usage Data: Credit transactions and subscription status

2. How We Use Your Information

  • To provide personalized astrology readings
  • To manage your account and credit balance
  • To maintain your reading history
  • To improve our services

3. AI Processing

When AI-powered readings are enabled, your input data (birth details, questions) may be sent to OpenAI's API for processing. We send only the minimum information needed for the reading. OpenAI's data usage is governed by their own privacy policy.

4. Data Storage

All your personal data is stored locally on your device using Android's Room database. We do not maintain cloud servers, external databases, or analytics services. Your data never leaves your device except when making optional AI API calls.

5. Third-Party Services

  • Google Sign-In: Used solely for authentication. We receive your name and email.
  • OpenAI API: Used for AI-generated readings when enabled. Subject to OpenAI's privacy policy.

6. Data Security

Your data is protected by Android's built-in app sandboxing. Passwords are hashed before storage. API keys are stored securely in the app configuration.

7. Your Rights (GDPR / Indian Data Protection)

  • Access: View all your data in the Profile section
  • Deletion: Delete all your data from Profile > Delete My Data
  • Portability: Your data is stored locally and can be accessed by you
  • Withdraw Consent: You can stop using the app at any time

8. Children's Privacy

AstroApp is not intended for children under 13 years of age. We do not knowingly collect information from children.

9. Changes to This Policy

We may update this privacy policy from time to time. Changes will be reflected in the "Last updated" date above.

10. Contact Us

For questions about this privacy policy or your data, contact us at: privacy@astroapp.example.com
